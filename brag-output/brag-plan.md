# Brag Plan: MemeForge

## 1. Project Planning Rubric

- **What is the product?** MemeForge: An AI Marketing Meme Studio with Engagement Prediction and Multimodal Gemini Visuals.
- **Who is it for?** Marketers, small businesses, startup founders, and social media creators who want high-engagement viral memes without spending hours guessing or publishing cringe content.
- **What is the core hook?** Stop making cringe marketing memes that get 3 likes. Use data-backed machine learning to predict engagement before rendering.
- **What is the reveal?** An end-to-end AI pipeline: Concept Generator $\rightarrow$ ML Predictor $\rightarrow$ Multimodal Gemini AI $\rightarrow$ PIL Typography Overlay.
- **What is the visual proof?** Real 75th-percentile virality distribution chart (30k+ upvotes) and live generated sample meme scoring 83.8% predicted engagement.
- **What is the tone?** `polished` + `confident` startup launch energy.
- **Duration:** Exactly 20.0 seconds (600 frames @ 30 FPS).
- **Audio selection:** `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (upbeat, driving electronic groove with 18.5s fade-out).
