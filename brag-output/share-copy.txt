=== LinkedIn Announcement ===

Meme marketing works because it feels native to social feeds—yet most teams waste hours guessing which concepts will land.

Excited to introduce MemeForge: An AI Marketing Meme Studio with Engagement Prediction.

Instead of guessing, MemeForge uses a three-tier architecture:
1. Brainstorms candidate concepts tailored to your product and tone.
2. An NLP classifier (TF-IDF + metadata signals) scores predicted engagement based on 10,000+ real memes.
3. Renders only the top-ranked concepts using Google Gemini AI and pixel-perfect PIL typography.

Full open-source codebase, EDA report, and Streamlit app available on GitHub:
